We're building a hexapod robotic platform designed to push the boundaries by solving the mobility limits of traditional wheeled robots. Domestic environments are full of everyday obstacles: stairs, thick carpets, thresholds, and cluttered floors, that require legged movement to navigate effectively. To build a complete, functional system, we are partnering with Guardians of the Hardware and integrating their TriArm manipulator. Our collaboration divides the complex problem of domestic cleanup into two focused engineering domains: our team is developing the hexapod chassis and navigation system to traverse challenging indoor terrain and locate debris, while Guardians of the Hardware provides the physical manipulation required to grip and remove it. By linking our mobility base with their robotic arm, we are developing a practical, go-anywhere robotic assistant capable of maintaining areas of the home that standard robotic vacuums simply cannot reach.
